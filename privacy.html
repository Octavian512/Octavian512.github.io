Privacy policy

Safety DOT Bot is a private tool of a trucking safety team. This page says what it does with data, including the access it is given to a Google account.

What the bot handles

Documents that team members send it in Telegram: roadside inspection reports and traffic tickets, which contain driver names, carrier names and numbers, and court dates.

Google account access

The team's Google account authorises the bot to use its Google Drive. The bot uses that access only to create folders, PDF files and spreadsheets inside the team's own folder, and to read and update those same files. It does not read, change or delete anything else in the Drive, and it does not share any of it with anyone.

Who else sees the data

Pages are read by Google Gemini to extract their fields, and messages travel through Telegram. Carrier numbers may be looked up in FMCSA's public records. Nothing is sold or given to anyone else.

Keeping and removing data

Records are kept in the team's own Drive and on the computer that runs the bot, for as long as the team needs them. The account owner can remove the bot's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the files from Drive.

Contact

Questions go to the team member who invited you to the bot.
